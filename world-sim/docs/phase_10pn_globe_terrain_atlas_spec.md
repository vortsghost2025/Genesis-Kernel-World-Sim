## 1. Context and Origin: The Double Disconnect of the World Atlas

On 2026-11-08, following the implementation of survival physics (thirst, spoilage, and tool condition in Phase 10PM), the operator evaluated the global map presentation in the 3D Civilization Observatory:

> *"the globe map itself i think is just the shell still the world landhasnt been done properly for th eetire world"*

Direct audit of `world-sim/viewer/prototype/index.html` and `world-sim/viewer/continent_terrain.json` confirms two structural mismatches:

### 1.1 Mismatch 1: Orbit/Globe Rendering is a Coarse, Generic Painted Proxy
1. **Under-resolved Geometry:** The planetary sphere mesh generated by `buildPlanetSurface()` (~line 1573) consists of only `PLANET_LON_STEPS = 360` by `PLANET_LAT_STEPS = 180` vertices ($64,800$ quadrilaterals). Each canonical continent patch occupies `GLOBE_CONTINENT_SPAN = 200.0 / GLOBE_RADIUS = 0.2222` radians ($\approx 12.73^\circ$). Across the $360^\circ$ sphere, each $200 \times 200$ continent gets only **$\approx 13 \times 13$ vertices** of spatial resolution. The high-resolution $259 \times 259$ subdivided ground terrain mesh is completely lost at orbit zoom.
2. **Disconnected Color Logic:** `planetLandColor(h, lat)` (~line 1530) ignores canonical biome data entirely. It colors the globe using 5 hardcoded latitude bands (`polar ice` for $|lat| > 1.24$, `boreal taiga` for $> 0.83$, `temperate green` for $> 0.52$, `arid belt` for $> 0.26$, `equatorial` below $0.26$), modulated only by altitude greying. The real canonical biomes (`forest`, `mountain`, `grassland`, `hills`, `river`, `lake`), which render so vividly in local views, do not exist on the globe.
3. **Fictional Atlas Landmarks:** `PLANET_LANDMARKS` (~line 1362) hardcodes 10 synthetic Gaussian landforms (e.g., mid-latitude continents at $(\pm 2.9, \pm 0.64)$, equatorial archipelagos, isolated island pairs) to keep the ocean from looking empty. These landmasses have no backing in `continent_terrain.json` or simulation state, creating a display shell that pretends the world has lands that agents can never reach.

### 1.2 Mismatch 2: The Underlying World Map Data is a Coarse Prototype Scaffold
1. **Square Boundary Boxes:** Both continents are currently represented as strict $200 \times 200$ coordinate boxes bounded by ocean rings (`canonical ocean` constitutes only $\sim 11.7\%$ of the tile array).
2. **Primitive Noise Distributions:** Coarse bilinear noise creates chunky rectangular biome clusters rather than natural geological cordilleras, river drainage networks, or rain-shadow biomes.
3. **Missing Planetary Climate Systems:** The world lacks true polar ice caps, tundra, arid rain-shadow basins, tropical rainforest belts, or realistic continental shelves.

## 3. Phase 10PQ Architectural Specification: Proper Globe Terrain Atlas

### 3.1 Procedural Planetary Canvas Rasterization (Client-Side, Zero Payload)
Rather than adding multi-megabyte static image textures to the deployment package, Phase 10PQ will implement an in-browser rasterizer (`generatePlanetTerrainAtlas()`) that renders a high-resolution $2048 \times 1024$ equirectangular texture directly onto an `OffscreenCanvas` (or standard `HTMLCanvasElement`) at initialization time:
1. **Base Ocean & Bathymetry Layer:**
   - Equirectangular projection mapping $(\lambda, \phi) \rightarrow (u, v)$ where $u = \lambda / (2\pi) \in [0, 1]$ and $v = (\phi + \pi/2) / \pi \in [0, 1]$.
   - Multi-tier ocean bathymetry: Deep Abyssal Trenches (`#041224`), Pelagic Ocean (`#0a223d`), Continental Slopes (`#123b61`), and Coastal Shallows/Reefs (`#1d5987`).
   - Polar ice caps naturally integrated at high latitudes ($|\phi| > 1.28$ rad).
2. **Canonical Continent Patch Rasterization:**
   - For each continent (`cont_a` at $\lambda = -0.85, \phi = 0.30$; `cont_b` at $\lambda = 0.85, \phi = -0.30$):
     - Compute the exact pixel bounding box on the $2048 \times 1024$ canvas corresponding to `GLOBE_CONTINENT_SPAN` ($0.2222$ rad).
     - Each $200 \times 200$ tile array maps to $\approx 72 \times 72$ canvas pixels. That is a major improvement over the current $\sim 13 \times 13$ vertex grid, but it is **not tile-for-tile resolution** (about 0.36 canvas pixel per tile across the 200-tile edge). The atlas is therefore a high-value cartographic color/biome layer, not a replacement for the high-resolution local mesh when zoomed in.
     - For every pixel within the patch, sample canonical `elev`, `terr`, `biome`, and `water` from `continent_terrain.json`.
3. **Biome Palette Color Mapping:**
   - **Lush Valley Pasture & Grassland:** `#4a7c2e` to `#5e9e38` with subtle perlin soil variance.
   - **Dense Conifer / Spruce Foothill Forests:** `#1a3d1c` to `#244d28` (matching the local 3D conifer forest belt).
   - **Dolomite Limestone Crags & Peaks:** Warm buff/gold rock `#9e8f78` with shadowed slate fissures `#424a52` and high-altitude snow crowns `#e8edf2` for tiles with $elev > 0.82$.
   - **Hydrology Ribbons & Lake Basins:** Glistening river ribbons (`#2b7da8`) and lake basins (`#20638a`).
   - **Coastal Sand & Shores:** Pale sandy beach fringes (`#c2b28f`) wherever land borders canonical ocean.

### 3.2 Dynamic Hillshade & Normal Mapping
To ensure the globe reads as a physical planetary sphere with rugged relief rather than a flat painted ball:
1. **Elevation Gradient Computation:**
   - Compute spatial derivatives $\frac{\partial h}{\partial x}$ and $\frac{\partial h}{\partial y}$ across neighboring pixels.
2. **Directional Hillshading:**
   - Apply Lambertian shading against a global orbital light vector $\vec{L} = \text{normalize}(-0.65, 0.55, 0.52)$.
   - Mountain ranges (like the Val di Funes / Odle Dolomite cordilleras on East and the Dawn crags on West) cast crisp, directional relief shadows across their northern and eastern flanks.
3. **Normal Map Texture (optional, not default):**
   - Bake gradients into an RGB normal map texture ($R = 0.5 + 0.5 \cdot n_x, G = 0.5 + 0.5 \cdot n_y, B = n_z$) only if it improves visual relief in A/B rendering.
   - Default path is color-space hillshade, because the planet geometry already carries normals; a baked normal map can double-shade or look dirty unless validated.

### 3.3 Cartographic Truth: Delineating Explored vs. Uncharted Expanse
To address the "fake extra continents" issue honestly:
1. **Canonical Continents:** Rendered with full topographic, biome, and hydrological fidelity.
2. **Uncharted Planetary Expanse:**
   - Retire the deceptive `PLANET_LANDMARKS` that pretend unmapped continents exist.
   - Style the vast ocean basin with cartographic bathymetric contour lines and depth/slope tints. These are decorative layers, not canonical simulation data, and must not imply playable land.
   - Any secondary procedural landmasses are removed in the canonical layer. If a legacy "fantasy atlas" toggle remains, it is not part of the canonical globe.

### 3.4 Camera Navigation & Continuous Zoom Pathway
1. **Multi-Scale Transition:**
   - **Global Orbit View ($R \ge 3.0 \cdot R_{\text{globe}}$):** Renders the full illuminated planetary sphere with atmospheric limb glow and cloud wisps.
   - **Continental Approach ($1.2 \cdot R_{\text{globe}} \le R < 3.0 \cdot R_{\text{globe}}$):** Camera orbits smoothly into the active continent patch (`cont_a` or `cont_b`).
   - **Regional / Valley Handoff ($R < 1.2 \cdot R_{\text{globe}}$):** Definite deterministic handoff trigger into the full $259 \times 259$ subdivided local 3D terrain mesh at a documented camera radius, with screenshot proof that the settlement stays centered and no clipping/pop-in occurs. A cross-fade is optional only if the prototype demonstrates it cleanly; hard morph/projection is acceptable if the handoff point is proven.

## 4. Test Suite Specification: `test_globe_terrain_atlas.py`

Candidate Phase 10PQ will implement and satisfy a comprehensive verification suite:

1. `test_globe_terrain_atlas_canvas_dimensions`:
   - Verifies the procedural atlas generator creates a power-of-two equirectangular canvas ($2048 \times 1024$ or $1024 \times 512$) with valid RGBA image data.
2. `test_canonical_continent_pixel_coordinates`:
   - Asserts that `cont_a` and `cont_b` spherical patch centers $(\lambda, \phi)$ map to exact, non-overlapping bounding boxes on the equirectangular projection.
3. `test_biome_color_fidelity`:
   - Samples pixels at known canonical coordinates (East valley settlement at $(0, 0)$, East Dolomite massif at $(0, -33)$, West Dawn coast at $(42, 50)$) and verifies colors match the canonical biomes rather than arbitrary latitude bands.
4. `test_elevation_gradient_hillshade`:
   - Verifies that mountain tiles produce non-zero elevation gradients and that the deterministic color shading path uses those gradients without requiring a separate normal texture.
5. `test_no_untracked_runtime_dependencies`:
   - Ensures texture generation runs headlessly without requiring external HTTP texture downloads or breaking `build_viewer_deploy.py`.
6. `test_temporal_dead_zone_and_const_resolution`:
   - Verifies all planetary atlas constants resolve in topological order without use-before-declaration errors.
